# Adit’s Code of Conduct

1. Engineering, above all else, is a **discipline**.
2. Hold **respect and authenticity** at the highest regard.
3. **Ferocious work ethic** for continuous self improvement.
   - **Learn! Learn! Learn!** → **Teach**.
4. Take **pride in your work**. Do work that makes you proud.
5. Distinguish sincere effort from outcome. **Honest efforts matter**.
6. Understand failure. Accept it. Take time to grieve, then **rebuild**.
7. Practise **accountability without excuses**.
8. **Prepare with discipline.** **Execute without hesitation.**
9. Ensure **team welfare** is above selfish welfare.
10. Practise **empathy driven leadership** but don’t forgo **honesty.**
11. Hold yourself to a **high standard**, and your leaders to an **even higher one**.
12. Whatever the role, **perform it at the highest possible level**.
13. Good talent + **poor attitude** = ?
14. **Details matter**.
15. Push boundaries in **pursuit of skill**.
16. Build a team that **has a conscience, that collaborates, that openly challenges and commits together**.
17. **Lead by example.** Show, don’t tell.
18. Aim to be at the right place at the right time. When you’re there, **speak up**.
19. Embrace the struggle but **know when to walk away**.
20. Seek the right kind of ambition. **Aim for growth**.
21. Argue from **first principles**.
